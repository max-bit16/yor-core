# high-end-visual-design

When using this skill:
1. Define the intended premium visual tone.
2. Increase refinement through typography, composition, materials, and restraint.
3. Remove anything cheap, loud, or generic.
4. Aim for a sophisticated and highly controlled result.

Output expectations:
- Emphasize premium visual cues.
- Keep the design elegant and intentional.
- Prefer quality of composition over quantity of effects.
